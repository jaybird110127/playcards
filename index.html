The Yamaha Playcard format

Songs on a strip of magnetic tape glued to the bottom of a sheet-music card, read by Yamaha keyboards from 1982 to 1985. The format was undocumented. This is the documentation.

  • Read the format specification The container, the header, the duration tracks, the pitch streams, the chord chart, the repeat structure, and how each of it was established.
  • The repository The tools that read a card, write one, play it and take it apart, and the rest of the documentation.

Decoded from the cartridge ROM and the cards themselves, not from Yamaha's patents. No Yamaha card images, ROM images or accompaniment patterns are published here.
